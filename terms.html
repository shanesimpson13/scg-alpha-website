Nothing trades unless you greenlight a specific coin. When you do, the automation watches that coin and buys and sells it according to parameters you set — the dip it waits for, the bounce that confirms it, your take-profit and stop levels.

This is a tool you operate, not a fund we manage. We exercise no discretion over which coins you trade or when you enter. Every position starts because you chose that coin and pressed the button. We do not select assets for you, and we do not manage a portfolio on your behalf.

The board shows coins that meet mechanical filters. That is a list, not a recommendation, and an appearance on it is not an opinion that a coin is worth buying.
