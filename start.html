01 · THE BOARD

It starts with what is actually moving.

The highest-volume coins on Solana, filtered on volume, liquidity and buy/sell balance before you ever see them. You are not scrolling a feed hoping to catch something.

02 · YOUR CALL

You pick the coin.

This is the only judgment the system will not make for you, and the only one worth making. Chart, holders, bundlers and the buy/sell read are all on the page when you choose.

03 · YOUR RULES

Set the rules once.

How far it must dip. How much it must reclaim. Where profit comes off and where the stop sits. Set them once and every coin you greenlight afterwards runs the same way.

04 · GREENLIGHT

Then you greenlight it.

One tap, and it shows you the exact levels that would trigger a buy before you commit to anything. From there you are done — the rest happens whether you are watching or not.

05 · THE WAIT

It waits for the dip.

This is the part people cannot do. It will sit through hours of a coin grinding sideways without buying, because nothing can trigger until price comes off the high it measured from.

06 · THE ENTRY

It buys the reclaim, not the knife.

A coin falling is not a reason to buy it. The buy only fires once price turns and takes back 5% off the low — the entry you talk yourself out of in the moment.

07 · THE POSITION

Targets are set before you are in.

Profit levels and the stop are written at entry, not decided while you are up and nervous. Nothing about the trade is renegotiated once it is running.

08 · THE EXIT

It takes the profit without you.

Half comes off at 2×, the rest rides to 4×, and the stop moves to your entry the moment the first half is out. You are not deciding whether to be greedy at the exact moment you are worst at it.
