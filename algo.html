Algorithmic Trading

01

What Is an Algorithmic System

An algorithmic system transforms inputs into decisions through defined logic. Consistency allows systems to operate without emotional bias.

02

Strategy Archetypes

Most strategies fall into a small number of categories. Understanding these patterns simplifies the design process.

03

Expectancy Frameworks

Profitability is determined by the relationship between win rate and payoff structure. Small edges compound when executed consistently.

04

Risk Frameworks

Survival enables iteration. Effective systems manage downside before optimizing upside.

05

Execution Reality

Markets are competitive environments. Latency, liquidity, and slippage influence real-world performance.

06

System Design

Clear structure reduces complexity. State machines allow strategies to respond predictably to changing conditions.

07

Iteration Process

Systems improve through measured adjustments. Small refinements often produce meaningful performance differences.
